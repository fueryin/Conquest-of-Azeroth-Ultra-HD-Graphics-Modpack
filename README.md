# 🌌 Ascension: Conquest of Azeroth – Ultra-HD Mod Pack

A complete **Ultra-HD graphics and audio overhaul** for **Ascension: Conquest of Azeroth**, bringing modernized creature models, high-resolution textures, enhanced spell effects, improved environments, lighting, water, shaders, and much more.

This mod pack combines numerous visual upgrades into one complete package, alongside a **custom 64-bit Conquest of Azeroth client**, **LuxVelum**, and my personal **ReShade preset**.

The goal is to dramatically improve the game's visual fidelity while preserving the original World of Warcraft art style.

## ⚠️ Compatibility Notice

**Fully supported on Conquest of Azeroth Core only.**

**Conquest Reborn, ArdenWoW, and Grimfall** currently detect modified clients. However, all HD patches, LuxVelum, ReShade, and other enhancements can still be used by renaming the patches.

## 🖼️ Extra Content

In addition to the HD patches, the download includes several additional enhancements.

**🖥️ 64-Bit Conquest of Azeroth Client (Required)**

- Custom 64-bit client included with the mod pack.
- Supports significantly more memory than the original 32-bit client.
- Better suited for extensive HD texture and model replacements.
- Required to run this overhaul.

**🌄 LuxVelum**

- Forever Fog enhancements.
- Improved environmental lighting.
- Enhanced water rendering.


**🎨 Custom ReShade Preset**

- My personal ReShade configuration, tuned for this overhaul.
- Improved visual clarity, contrast, colors, and overall presentation.

**⚙️ Custom Config.wtf – Maximum Graphics**

My personal Config.wtf file with graphics settings pushed to their maximum.

Preconfigured for the best possible visual quality, including enhanced draw distances, environmental detail, shadows, and graphical effects.

Included for those who want to get the most out of the Ultra-HD overhaul without manually adjusting console variables.

⚠️ Important: Please open Config.wtf and adjust the resolution and refresh rate to match your monitor's settings. It is currently configured for 3840 × 2160 240HZ.

**🎵 Additional Add-ons**

Blood Splats on Critical Hits – Adds blood splatter effects on-screen when landing critical strikes.

Dance Music – Adds music when using dance emotes.

## 📥 Installation
1. Download and extract the **[Ultra-HD Mod Pack](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1Nm4fZt_aUcGdi0KGaGX7PX6K1swsf2m3?usp=sharing)**.
2. Move the included files into your **Conquest of Azeroth client folder**.
3. Launch your game using the **CoA64.exe**



### 💡 How to Bypass the Google Drive Download Limit

If Google Drive reports that the download quota has been exceeded:

1. Add the file as a shortcut to your Google Drive.
2. Create a new folder in your Drive and move the shortcut into it.
3. Download the folder as a ZIP archive.

This workaround may help when Google Drive restricts direct downloads.
