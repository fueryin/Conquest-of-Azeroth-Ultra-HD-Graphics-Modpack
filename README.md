## 🖼️ Extra Content

In addition to the HD patches, the download includes several additional enhancements.

**🖥️ 64-Bit Conquest of Azeroth Client (Required)**

- Custom 64-bit client included with the mod pack.
- Significantly more performance than the original 32-bit client.
- Required to run this overhaul.

**🌄 LuxVelum**

- Forever Fog, Lighting, & Water enhancements.
- Press CTRL+F7 to configure in-game

**🎨 Custom ReShade Preset**

- Improves visual clarity, contrast, colors, and overall presentation.
- Press HOME to configure in-game

⚙️ **Custom Config.wtf**

- Maximized draw distance, environmental detail, shadows, and effects beyond in-game graphics options.

**Important:**
- Open `Config.wtf` and adjust the resolution, refresh rate, and UI scale to your preference.
- Avoid changing graphics settings in-game; use `Config.wtf` to change graphics.
- If changed in-game, max FPS resets to 15 and must be adjusted back.
- You MUST keep Specular Lighting Enabled in graphic settings.

**🎵 Additional Add-ons**

- Blood Splats on Critical Hits – Adds blood splatter effects on-screen when landing critical strikes.
- Dance Music – Adds music when using dance emotes.
